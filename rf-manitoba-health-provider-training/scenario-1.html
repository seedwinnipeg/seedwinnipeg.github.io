Dana's Disability Tax Credit form

Fictional case. Dana is 52. She had a stroke eight months ago and has weakness on her right side. She gets tired quickly and can't manage a full day. She's no longer working. At a follow-up visit, she asks you to complete the medical part of the Disability Tax Credit form (T2201).

What do you do first?

  • Fill in the form today, using her diagnosis and hospital discharge summary
  • Book a longer visit to assess how her condition affects her daily activities
  • Tell her she likely won't qualify, because she can still walk
