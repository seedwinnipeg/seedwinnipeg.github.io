Questions from other providers

Questions asked by learners and answered by the presenters. Identifying details are removed before anything is published.

  • What should I focus on when describing a client's restrictions on the DTC form?

    Sample answer: Describe how the condition affects day-to-day function (what the person can't do, or needs much longer to do, and how often), not only the diagnosis.
