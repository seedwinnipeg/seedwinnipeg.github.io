Content type: text and callout

Many programs, one entry point

Disability benefits in Canada come from both the federal and provincial governments. Each program has its own eligibility rules, forms and timelines. Several of them depend on one another, so a single missed step can close off several benefits at once.

Key point

Health providers are often the only people who can complete the medical part of an application. Your form can decide whether a client gets several benefits, not just one.

Tax filing and the DTC open the door

What comes first

  1. File a tax return every year, even with little or no income
  2. Get approved for the Disability Tax Credit (DTC), which needs a health provider to certify form T2201

→

What it can unlock (sample)

  • Canada Disability Benefit (CDB)
  • Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP), with government grants and bonds
  • Child Disability Benefit, for families
