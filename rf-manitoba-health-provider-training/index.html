Theme

Disability benefits: helping your clients apply

A 90-minute course for Manitoba health providers, including physicians, nurses, nurse practitioners, social workers, dietitians, dentists, occupational and physical therapists, and pharmacists.

Co-delivered by an expert health provider, a community financial advice expert, and a person with lived experience of disability and living on a low income.

By the end, you'll be able to

  • Explain why helping clients access disability benefits matters for their health
  • Describe Manitoba and federal disability benefit programs: eligibility, applications, forms and appeals
  • Recognize the barriers, and what helps, when you support clients to apply
